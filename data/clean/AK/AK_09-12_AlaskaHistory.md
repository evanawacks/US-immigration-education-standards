## Issues

Adopted-AK-SS-Standards-2024_09c1fb.pdf: Anchor-standard label cells are split across many cells and pages (e.g. 'Civics Anchor' / 'Standard 6' / 'Civic and Political' / 'Institutions and' / 'Systems'); they were labeled standards with the band (K-2, 3-5) or the grade/course of the table, and are interleaved with leveled cells on continuation pages (the grade-band standard in C2 is repeated at the top of a page when its leveled rows continue).
Adopted-AK-SS-Standards-2024_09c1fb.pdf: K-2 and 3-5 inquiry standards (u392-u431, u977-u1025) and 6-8 inquiry standards (u1666-u1715) are band-level skills standards; labeled with the full band (K-2, 3-5, 6-8) so they are copied into each grade. 9-12 inquiry (u2552-u2627) labeled course null, grades 9-12.
Adopted-AK-SS-Standards-2024_09c1fb.pdf: Grade 6, 7, 8 are grade-level (course null) even though each has a thematic title (Grade 6 Alaska Studies and Geography; Grade 7 World History and Geography; Grade 8 U.S. History and Civics). Grades 9-12 discipline sections (Civics, Economics, Geography, Alaska History, U.S. History, World History) were treated as courses per the state guide, course = discipline name from the section title ('Grade 9 through 12 Civics Standards' -> 'Civics'); the section headings u2629, u2691, u2743, u2787, u3154, u3624 are labeled with that course.
Adopted-AK-SS-Standards-2024_09c1fb.pdf: u2788-u2800 (Alaska History time periods and timeframes table) labeled standards, course 'Alaska History' (course-level organizing framework, analogous to grade themes); could alternatively be considered examples/backup.
