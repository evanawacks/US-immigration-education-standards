SSP.06
3 | THIRD GRADE SOCIAL STUDIES The History of the United States: Early Americans to Westward Expansion Course Description: Third grade students will learn about Indigenous people of North America, European exploration, early American settlements, and the founding of the Thirteen Colonies. Students will also develop an understanding of the events that led to the U.S. Independence, including the American Revolution and the formation of the national government under the Constitution. Students will continue their studies with the development and growth the United States through Manifest Destiny.
