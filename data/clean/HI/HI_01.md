## Examples / clarifications

Sample Compelling
• What makes a good community? • Where are we?
Questions
• Democratic Values: common good, equality, freedom, liberty, popular sovereignty, respect for individual rights
• Rules: positive comments, respect for others, taking turns • Listening: eyes on speaker, refrain from talking, ask thoughtful questions
• Maps: physical, political • Map Features: cardinal and ordinal directions, compass rose, equator, legends, title, scale, symbols
Sample Compelling
• What is the best way to manage limited resources? • How does our environment affect how we live?
Questions
• Natural Resource: materials that occur in nature and can be used by people (e.g., clean air, energy, timber, water) • Human Resource: people with skills and knowledge to produce goods or supply services (e.g., artists, cafeteria workers, construction workers, teachers) • Capital Resource: goods used to make other goods and services (e.g., classroom tools, machines)
• Physical Features: deserts, mountains, rainforests, rivers, valleys, ocean ridges and trenches • Continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, South America • Oceans: Arctic, Atlantic, Indian, Pacific, Southern
• Cultural Characteristics: art, clothing, customs, foods, housing, language, recreation, religion • Environmental Characteristics: animals, climate, forests, islands, landmarks, plants, reefs
• Climate: humidity, precipitation, seasons, temperature, topography, wind • Weather and Natural Disasters: drought, earthquake, hurricanes, precipitation, rising sea levels, tsunami, volcanic eruption
• Community Improvement: help your neighbors, plant a community garden, run for office, volunteer • Environmental Improvement: beautification projects, buy local, clean ups, reduce, reuse, recycle
Sample Compelling
• Why should we learn about historical events? • Where can we find out about history?
Questions
• Events: Gutenberg invents printing press, Mayflower lands at Plymouth Rock, Boston Tea Party, attack on Pearl Harbor • People: Genghis Khan, Leonardo Da Vinci, George Washington, Benjamin Franklin, Kamehameha, Abraham Lincoln, Prince Kūhiō, Marie Curie, Wright Brothers, Rosa Parks, Neil Armstrong • Observances: Independence Day, Veterans Day, Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Statehood Day, Cultural Holidays (e.g., Kamehameha Day, Chinese New Year, May Day, Flag Day, Girls’ Day, Boys’ Day)
• Facts: dates, names, places, statistics • Opinion: conjecture, interpretations, personal feelings
• Comparisons: clothing, food, jobs and roles, technology, transportation
Sample Compelling
• How do you make choices? • Why can’t we get everything we want?
Questions
• Goods: tangible items, (e.g., food, games, toys) • Services: work done for others to provide something needed or wanted, (e.g., carpenter, plumber)
• Currency: gold, money, silver • Barter: exchange services or goods of equal value • Trade: exchange goods for money
• Causes: likes and dislikes, personal and family choices made, the need to follow rules, unexpected events • Events: breaking a rule, earning an award, moving to another place, participating in new activities, personal and family celebrations
