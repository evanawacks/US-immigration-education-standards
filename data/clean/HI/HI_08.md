• Structure: executive, judicial, and legislative branches, federalism • Powers: appropriations, checks and balances, declarations of war, impeachment, judicial review, legislation, veto
• Types of Liberties: civil liberties, criminal law protections, political freedoms, religious freedom

• Citizenship: 1790 Alien Naturalization Act, Native Americans • Voting Rights: free Blacks, Jacksonian Democracy, property qualifications, women
• Factions to Parties: Anti-Federalists, Democratic- Republicans, Federalists, Jacksonian Democrats, Whigs • Dividing Issues: state debts, Whiskey Rebellion, Alien and Sedition Acts, War of 1812, Panic of 1819, Second Bank of the United States
