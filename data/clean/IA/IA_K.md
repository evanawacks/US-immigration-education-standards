## Examples / clarifications

Kindergarteners will learn to ask and answer questions about a topic using different sources such as books, pictures, maps, and interviews. They will practice sharing facts and opinions about what they have learned through talking, drawing, or writing. There will also be learning about ways to participate within communities. Students will explore various communities they belong to, compare how life today is different from the past, describe how and why people move from place to place, and create simple routes to specific locations. Additionally, kindergartners will learn about rules and responsibilities for a variety of settings. Finally, they will identify needs versus wants, understand basic money concepts like buying and borrowing, and make thoughtful choices about spending.
