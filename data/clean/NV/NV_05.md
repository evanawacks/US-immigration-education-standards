## Examples / clarifications

Grade 5: The U.S. - Creating a New Nation In fifth grade, students learn about European exploration of North America, the intersection and conflict among Native, European, and African cultures, and the colonization of North America. In addition, students study the American Revolution and investigate the foundational documents of the United States, including the Declaration of Independence, U.S. Constitution, and Bill of Rights. Using an array of source materials, fifth grade students explore, analyze and critique individual rights and responsibilities in the United States. They learn about the important historical events and diverse actors of the American Colonies, Revolution, and the New Nation. Students study how culture shapes laws, how laws ensure rights and responsibilities for the people who live within a society and discuss how these ideas manifest today.
