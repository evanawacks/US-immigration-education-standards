## Examples / clarifications

Kindergarten: Building Community – Learning & Working Together In kindergarten, students learn how to work together in a productive classroom community with rights and responsibilities. In addition, students engage in understanding how individuals learn and work together in the school and classroom community. A focus on rights and responsibilities of learning and working together in the school or classroom community frames discussions and tasks.
