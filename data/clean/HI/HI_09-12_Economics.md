## Examples / clarifications

Sample Performance
The student: Uses current personal or family budgets, goals, and values to describe how marginal costs and marginal benefits are related to economic decisions.
Sample Performance
The student: Identifies the factors of production in coffee manufacturing and explains how these factors affect its production.
Sample Performance
The student: Uses the laws of supply and demand to explain how changes in the price of oil may affect the supply of alternative sources of energy.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains how nations with different currencies are able to buy and sell goods and services globally.
Sample Performance
The student: Describes a local entrepreneur or a historically significant entrepreneur and explains how his or her innovation or methods of production contributed to the growth of the community or nation.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains the impact of a bankruptcy of a major corporation on the performance of the stock market.
Sample Performance
The student: Develops a financially solvent retirement plan for people in various careers.
Sample Performance
The student: Identifies the trade-offs and opportunity costs of buying a new car.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains the importance of banking and how it serves as a medium of exchange in a market-based economy.
Sample Performance
The student: Describes how the number of sellers of a similar good or service in a market affects the quality, quantity, and price of those goods or services.
Sample Performance
The student: Uses various measures of economic health (such as GDP, CPI, income statistics) to assess the economic health of the nation.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains how monetary and fiscal policy are used to control inflation.
Sample Performance
The student: Uses data on taxes to explain government revenue sources.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains how the foreign exchange market affects the cost of an imported skateboard.
Sample Performance
The student: Explains how trade agreements affect economic development in partner nation(s).
Sample Performance
The student: Compares a capitalist nation to a communist nation in terms of economic advantages and disadvantages.
