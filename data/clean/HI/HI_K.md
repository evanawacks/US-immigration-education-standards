## Examples / clarifications

Sample Compelling Questions
• Why do we need rules? • Why do rules sometimes change?
• Rules: classroom, library, playground, home, park, store • Consequences: disciplinary action, increased or revoked privileges, rewards
• Working Together: active listening, cooperation, equal access to resources, group rules, inclusion of all voices
• Role of Self: classmate, community member, family member, friend • Roles of Others: classmates, employees, family members, friends, neighbors, police officers, teachers • Responsibilities: active participation, chores, enforcing rules, following rules, respect for yourself and others, work duties
Sample Compelling
• How does what you do affect others? • Why do people see things differently?
Questions
• Time: today, yesterday, long ago, before, after, younger, older, beginning, middle, end, days, weeks, months • Events: birthdays, family functions, family travel, holidays
• Events: celebrated a birthday, celebrated a holiday, made a new friend, played a game, visited family, went shopping • Effects: felt happy, gave someone a gift, received a gift, tasted new food, won or lost a game
• Initial Understanding of Point of View: daily activities, fairy tale characters’ perspectives, sympathy, perspective, proximity to situation
Sample Compelling
• How does where we live affect how we live? • Why can’t we have everything we need and want?
Questions
• Scarcity: in the classroom (e.g., supplies, technology), at home (e.g., toys, money, living space), in the community (e.g., playground, parks, police) • Choices: conserve, give, pay more, save, share
• Differences: often determined by different perspectives and values • Needs: air, water, food, shelter • Wants: phone, television, toys
• Physical Features: cities, mountains, oceans, roads, volcanoes • Positional Words: above, below, near, far, left, right, and cardinal directions
• How: animal, bike, boat, bus, car, jitney, plane, subway, train, walk, ship • Why: adventure, job, school, war, weather
