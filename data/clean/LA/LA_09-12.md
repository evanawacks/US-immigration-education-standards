## Standards

9-12
9-12.SP1. Examine sources in order to: a.​ Distinguish between primary, secondary, and tertiary sources. b.​ Determine the origin, author's point of view, intended audience, and reliability. c.​ Analyze the meaning of words, phrases, and content-specifci vocabulary.
9-12.SP2. Use a variety of primary and secondary sources to: a.​ Analyze social studies content. b.​ Evaluate claims, counterclaims, and evidence. c.​ Compare and contrast multiple sources and accounts. d.​ Explain how the availability of sources affects historical interpretations.
9-12.SP3. Construct and express claims that are supported with relevant evidence from primary and/or secondary sources, social studies content knowledge, and clear reasoning and explanations to: a.​ Demonstrate an understanding of social
studies content. b.​ Compare and contrast content and viewpoints. c.​ Analyze causes and effects. d.​ Evaluate counterclaims.
