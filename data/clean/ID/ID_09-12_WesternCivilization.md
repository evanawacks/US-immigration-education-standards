## Backup (non-standards text)

CONTEXT
As a part of the revisions to Social Studies, a committee of educators and subject matter experts from across the state came together to create a consistent set of content standards for a course in Western Civilization. These standards are now available for schools and districts that choose to offer it. The standards span from 800 BC to 1815 AD and focus on the foundations of Western Civilization, offering guidance on key ideas, events, and figures that shaped the modern Western world.
